# PDF Viewer

Read PDFs in the game and show your players the pages you pick. They get only those pages, never the
whole file. Everyone can highlight, draw, pin notes and reply, and players can follow your scrolling.
Addon key `pdf`.

## Using it

| Where | What |
|---|---|
| **Cards → Templates → PDF Document** (GM) | Make a card from it and open it. Pick a PDF from the Materials panel, or upload one in the card. |
| The document (GM) | The whole PDF. Type pages (`3`, `3-5, 8`) and a title, then **Show to players**: the pages open for everyone. **Present** makes players who follow you scroll with you, on the pages they have. Under **Shown to players**: jump to a share, **take back** a share, **open for everyone** again. |
| The player window | One tab per share. **Follow GM** keeps you where the GM is; untick it to look around. |
| Tools (everyone) | **Highlight** (select text), **Marker** (drag over an area), **Box**, **Pin** (click), **Draw**, and a colour. **Select** a note to add text, reply, or delete it. |

- Only the note's author or a GM can change or delete it; anyone who sees the pages can reply.
- Notes belong to pages, not to shares: a page shown again shows its notes again.
- Notes can only go on pages that are shown.

## What players get

When you show pages, your browser cuts them into a new, small PDF and stores it in the game under a
random name. Players download only that file (the app keeps it in its cache), so the rest of the book
stays with you. Copied pages carry only the images they draw: nothing from other pages, and none of the
page's own annotations, links or metadata.

Some PDFs, often bought ones, are encrypted against copying. Their pages are sent as pictures
(rendered at twice their size), so players can't select text on them: they highlight with the Marker
instead.

The **PDF Document** card is never shared, and it alone knows where the whole file is. Players read the
document's **PDF Presentation** (made the first time you show pages, in the `PDF` folder of the Cards
panel): the shares, where you are, and the notes.

## Limits

Up to 200 pages per share and 2000 notes per PDF. A note's text can be up to 2000 characters, and a
drawing up to 1500 points.

Opening a large PDF for the first time takes a while: it travels over the game connection once, and then
it comes from your browser's cache.
